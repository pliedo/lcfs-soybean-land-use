# Preliminary linehaul cost exposure grid

Estimated 36960 coefficient rows; 1344 unidentified models. All estimated specifications are exported.

These use incomplete linehaul proxies, not validated full transport costs. Gulf routes apply only to Rodeo in this grid; other plants retain direct rail. Alternatives are separate scenarios, never shipment shares. Access-screen exclusions are eligibility assumptions.

Crusher and year fixed effects; crusher-clustered intervals. Current, lag1/2/3, distributed0–2, lead diagnostic, hub trends, exclude2019, and California/other decomposition. Shares, log and level soybean acres. 2025 capacity is provisional. Offsets50 and decay100 truck-equivalent km are transparent assumed sensitivity scales. Nearest allocation splits tied minima evenly. Simultaneous physical refinery shocks are summed once per plant.

Nominal p-values are not adjusted for the many specifications. Observational coefficients are not established causal effects. Leave-one-out influence, balanced-support and forest-break diagnostics remain outstanding; do not treat this report as the final requested diagnostic report.
