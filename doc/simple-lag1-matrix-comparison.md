# Simple lag-one matrix comparison

Model: Y_hrt = alpha_hr + gamma_rt + beta_r E_h,t-1 + error_hrt. E is the sum of physical-refinery planting-aligned eligible capacity times refinery-to-crusher weights, in 100 million gallons/year units. Six separate ring slopes in one stacked model; crusher–ring and ring–year fixed effects; crusher-clustered 95% intervals. Share outcome is soybean / non-water valid pixels (codes 83,111,81 excluded); acres are estimated separately. Water correction does not establish fixed land support or causal identification.

All matrix/weight comparisons use the same 2015–2025 lag-one sample. Primary scenario physical_onset_snapshot_capacity; disputed-site exclusion also retained in coefficient/contrast outputs. No raster extraction repeated. Matrix alternatives: actual road, straight-line screen benchmark, documented-mode cost proxy, and half/double rail freight rates with constrained route reselection. Cost proxies use baseline truck-equivalent km; road/air use km. These are modelling assumptions, not procurement shares. Three weights: refinery-normalized inverse square (50 km offset), normalized exponential (100 km scale), and cheapest eligible allocation splitting ties. All refineries retained; no geographic cutoff.

Average 100 mgy addition averages equally across 16 existing physical refinery locations, not an estimated national representative opening. Cumulative effects sum acre predictions from first two/three exclusive rings; aggregate share is area weighted. Full cross-ring covariance retained. 2024 non-water area is held fixed for illustrative translation. Coefficient uncertainty excludes transport, capacity, and land-measurement uncertainty. Multiple tests are nominal, unadjusted.

Baseline 2024 share: 0–50 miles 21.4002338%; 0–75 miles 18.3292453%. Road inverse-square lag-one: +0.0007320 pp (0–50), p=.04437; +0.0004526 pp (0–75), p=.18748. Direct acres: +1248.9 (p=.11419), +1070.5 (p=.43457). Share-implied acres +1431.2 and +1521.1 are different estimands from direct acreage regressions. Baseline mode-cost inverse-square estimates are positive but insignificant in both footprints.

The prior significant conditional lag-one coefficient came from the joint lead/current/lag model on 2016–2023. A lag-one-only model does not inherit its coefficient or significance. Planting-aligned current exposure already accounts for approval cutoff and prior capacity; CDL observes crops during that crop year, not one year after planting. Lag one tests an additional one-crop-year delay. A persistent capacity addition affects future exposure as well; coefficients are exploratory associations, not established policy effects.
