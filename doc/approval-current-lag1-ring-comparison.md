# Approval-year current plus lag-one comparison

Reproduce with code/55_compare_approval_current_lag1.py and code/56_plot_approval_ring_effects.py. All previous results preserved; no raster recomputation.

Stacked model: Y_hrt = crusher-ring FE + ring-year FE + beta_0r E_ht + beta_1r E_h,t-1 + error. All six rings estimated jointly; crusher-clustered intervals, full covariance for current-plus-lag-one and cumulative contrasts. Same 2015–2025 sample, 3212 observations and 57 clusters. All five matrix definitions and three weighting methods estimated for both outcomes and both crosswalk scenarios; zero rank failures. Results are exploratory, nominal p-values without multiple-testing adjustment.

Approval-year exposure uses annual capacity stock times eligibility in the first observed soybean approval calendar year, with no May cutoff. It assigns full annual exposure in that year regardless of approval month; a timing approximation, not observed demand. Existing planting-aligned exposure retained as comparison: May cutoff and previous-calendar-year capacity. Consequently the comparison changes both eligibility timing and capacity dating, not only a relabelled year. 2025 capacity held at 2024 level, known approvals only; no new 2025 approval history inferred. Approval dates are first-observed in compiled records, not independently verified legal effective dates for every plant.

Exposure sums capacity over physical refineries once, weighted toward crushers. Primary figure uses refinery-normalized inverse-square weighting with 50-unit offset, road km or mode-cost truck-equivalent km. Coefficients are per 100 mgy weighted crusher exposure, not per 100 mgy refinery opening. Separate acreage outcome is in acres; share is percentage points of non-water valid pixels. Individual ring slopes are not cumulative predictions.

For a permanent exposure step: first season response beta0*x; next and later seasons (beta0+beta1)*x. Lag-one coefficient alone is conditional on current exposure and is not this net prediction. Correlated persistent exposure can produce opposite-signed coefficients; neither establishes displacement or delayed causality by itself. No leads in this model: no-anticipation is an assumption, not a tested finding.

Road inverse-square share lag-one coefficients rings 1–6: +0.227749, +0.210588, +0.186679, +0.097177, +0.060023, -0.031105 pp; nominal p .000985, .002638, .000740, .028444, .130067, .242178. Current coefficients are negative in every ring. Sums in first three rings +.026430, +.003515, -.025650 pp, all insignificant. Outer sums negative and significant. Ring slope significance does not test differences between rings; plot does not assert pairwise slope differences. Complete CSVs preserve all signs and matrices, including disputed-site exclusions.
