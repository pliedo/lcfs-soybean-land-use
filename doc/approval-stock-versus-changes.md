# Approval stock versus annual changes

Capacity eligibility is the policy exposure; no credit price, CI, revenue or dollars enter regressions. Approval-year stock counts each physical refinery once after first observed soybean approval. Additional pathways do not duplicate existing capacity. Annual changes are signed differences of this stock, including eligibility additions, capacity expansions and reductions, not an approval-only instrument. A separate expansion is not necessarily a new policy approval. Dates remain compiled first-observed approvals. Annual stock assigns capacity in the approval calendar year without month weighting. 2025 holds 2024 capacity and known eligibility only.

code/57_compare_approval_stock_changes.py estimates lag-one-only and current-plus-lag-one for both definitions, both crosswalk scenarios, five matrices, three weighting rules, six rings, two outcomes. Common 2016–2025 sample chosen because 2014 initial stock changes cannot be inferred without 2013 stock; no initial stock falsely coded as new approval. Original outputs preserved; no raster processing. Crusher-ring and ring-year FE; crusher-clustered covariance and full cross-ring contrasts. Code58 plots ring-level lag-one coefficients, not hypothetical refinery cumulative effects.

Stock-only lag one predicts a sustained next-season association for a sustained stock increase. Annual-change lag one predicts an outcome-level response in the following year to a one-time eligible-capacity increment; it does not mechanically imply permanent acreage changes. Neither coefficient alone is causal. Multiple comparisons use unadjusted nominal p-values. All identified coefficients preserved, no sign-based selection. Reductions retained rather than treated as approvals. Nonwater shares remain coverage-dependent, not fixed-mask outcomes.
